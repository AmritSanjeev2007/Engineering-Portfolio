University collaborative project

Ping-Pong Ball Launcher

  • CAD design & simulationRole
  • 2025 – 2026When
  • Autodesk Inventor, simulationTools

Overview

A collaborative university project to build a ping-pong ball launcher. The mechanism uses two horizontal counter-rotating flywheels for propulsion and allows control over the yaw and pitch of the projectile's direction.

What I did

  • Designed most of the complex parts in CAD (Autodesk Inventor).
  • Computationally simulated the flywheel and steering mechanisms before manufacture.
  • Helped organise and delegate tasks across the team to keep the build efficient.

What I learned

Collaborating on a large, complicated project; problem-solving to design components that deliver the required function; and refining mechanisms through software-human feedback loops between simulation and the physical build.
