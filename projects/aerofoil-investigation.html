IB Extended Essay · Physics

Investigating Aerofoils

  • Independent researcherRole
  • 2024 – 2025When
  • Experimental setup, data analysisTools

Overview

My 3000-word IB Physics Extended Essay investigated the effect of applying loads to a flexible, homogeneous aerofoil, to simulate the stresses and resulting strain it would experience in real flight.

What I did

  • Designed the loading experiment on a flexible aerofoil.
  • Measured and analysed the resulting strain under different loads.
  • Wrote up the method, results and evaluation as a formal 3000-word essay.

What I learned

Independent and efficient learning: scoping a research question, teaching myself the relevant structural mechanics, and seeing a long investigation through on my own.
